      How to read the map
      Each tower is one parking lot
      Its height is the cars still in it (1,000 cars is about 200 m tall). It shrinks as the lot empties.
      Amber: drivers in their car, trying to get out
      Engine running, not moving yet. This is the time we want to cut.
      Blue: card holders staying on purpose
      They took the $50 Visa card and wait in the stadium or at Patriot Place until their lot opens.
      Grey: not at their car yet
      Still in their seats or walking to the lot.
      Bars along Route 1: the queue
      One bar per 40 m of road; each side is one direction. Green is moving, tall and red is packed (walking pace).
      Red ring: a gate that can't let anyone out
      The road outside is full, so the lot is stuck. The red line points to the stretch of Route 1 blocking it.
      Blue tags: lots that got cards in this plan
